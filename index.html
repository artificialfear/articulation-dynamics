Dynamics Warm-up — touch every level, then shape the sound (plays in time)

#1: every level from pp up to ff and back down — play each half note, rest on the half rest. #2: the shapes — fp (loud attack, soft right away) < f, f > p, and p < f > p on one breath. Use #1 to introduce dynamics, then both as a daily warm-up before mixing and matching. Turn on Show loudness to see each level as a step and each hairpin as a ramp.
